# LAN, WLAN and WAN

## LAN

LAN stands for **Local Area Network**.

A LAN connects computers and other devices within a limited geographical area.

Examples:

- School computer lab
- Office network
- Home network

Example:

PC 1 ----\
PC 2 ----- Switch
PC 3 ----/

The devices can communicate with each other within the local network.

---

## WLAN

WLAN stands for **Wireless Local Area Network**.

A WLAN provides network connectivity using wireless communication, usually Wi-Fi.

Example:

Laptop  ))))
Phone   ))))  Wi-Fi Router
PC      ))))

WLAN is commonly used in homes, offices, schools, and public places.

---

## WAN

WAN stands for **Wide Area Network**.

A WAN connects networks over a large geographical area.

Examples include:

- Networks connecting different offices in different cities
- Networks connecting different regions or countries
- The Internet

---

## Comparison

| Type | Full Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| LAN | Local Area Network | School computer lab |
| WLAN | Wireless Local Area Network | Wi-Fi network |
| WAN | Wide Area Network | Internet |

---

## Simple Example

A school may have a local network connecting computers:

Computer Lab
      |
     LAN
      |
School Network
      |
    Router
      |
   Internet
      |
     WAN

The LAN is used for local communication, while the WAN can connect the school network to networks outside the local area.

---

## Key Learning Points

- **LAN** is used for a local area.
- **WLAN** is a wireless local network.
- **WAN** covers a much larger geographical area.
- The Internet is an example of a very large interconnected network.

---

## Learning Note

Understanding LAN, WLAN, and WAN is an important foundation for learning computer networking and system integration.
