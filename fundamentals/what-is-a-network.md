# What Is a Network?

## Definition

A computer network is a group of devices connected together so they can communicate and share resources.

Examples of network devices include:

- Computers
- Laptops
- Printers
- Smartphones
- Servers
- Routers
- Switches

---

## Why Are Networks Used?

Networks allow users and devices to:

- Share files
- Share printers
- Access the internet
- Communicate with other devices
- Access network services
- Share resources
